# Terms of Use for Academic Evidence Highlighter

Effective date: June 4, 2026

These Terms of Use describe acceptable use of Academic Evidence Highlighter.

## Purpose

Academic Evidence Highlighter is a research-assistance tool. It helps users locate candidate evidence in scholarly search results, article pages, and PDFs. It is designed to support human review, not replace human judgment.

## Candidate Evidence Only

The extension may produce incorrect, incomplete, or irrelevant results. Any highlight, summary, confidence label, or extracted answer term should be treated as candidate evidence only.

Users are responsible for checking the original source before relying on any result in academic, professional, legal, medical, scientific, or other important work.

## User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

- Verifying evidence against the original source.
- Following academic integrity rules, citation standards, and research ethics.
- Respecting copyright law, publisher terms, paywalls, licenses, and institutional access rules.
- Using exported CSV files responsibly.
- Not using the extension to copy, redistribute, or republish copyrighted works without permission or a valid legal basis.

## No Paywall Bypass

The extension does not bypass paywalls, subscriptions, login requirements, DRM, or publisher access controls. It can only scan content that the user's browser can access or files the user manually selects.

## No Professional Advice

The extension does not provide legal, medical, financial, scientific, or academic advice. It is a productivity tool for finding text that may be relevant to a user's own research question.

The extension's code, name, icons, and documentation are owned by the extension developer unless otherwise stated.

## Changes

The extension may change over time. These terms and the privacy policy should be updated when features change in a way that affects user data, permissions, or user responsibilities.
